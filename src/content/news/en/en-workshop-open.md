Filius on iPad is not appearing in one giant leap. Many small decisions shape the app, its documentation, learning materials, and the infrastructure around it. This journal will make that work easier to follow.

## Three kinds of update

Every entry carries a status. **Development** covers decisions and progress that are already concrete. **Preview** shows work that may still be reviewed, changed, or discarded. **Release** is reserved for changes that are genuinely available.

That distinction lets a preview be interesting without quietly becoming a roadmap commitment. We will publish dates only when they are dependable.

## More than a changelog

A list of version numbers rarely explains why something was built. The journal therefore leaves room for context:

- Which classroom situation are we trying to improve?
- Which differences between iPad and desktop need deliberate design?
- How can we protect data without making support unnecessarily difficult?
- Which drafts still need subject, technical, or legal review?

> Useful development notes show not only what changed, but the question that led to the change.

## Feedback is part of the process

The journal is not intended as a one-way broadcast. Classroom experience, support questions, and accessibility feedback should shape future entries. Feedback can be sent through the [support page](../../support/).
