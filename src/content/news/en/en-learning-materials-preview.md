A successful ping is a satisfying first result. A classroom sequence needs more after that: clear learning goals, observable intermediate steps, reflection questions, and a way to review results together.

## One package with several parts

The current draft connects three formats:

1. a printable workbook for learners,
2. a guide with sequencing and notes for teachers,
3. a Moodle learning path with a question bank and prepared SCORM structure.

The material follows the same basic flow as the quick start: design a topology, configure devices, start the simulation, and inspect traffic. Later tasks are intended to move gradually toward services and troubleshooting.

## Why there are no downloads yet

The files are deliberately not published on the website. Content, accessibility, export quality, licensing notes, and classroom use all require review first. The Moodle and SCORM parts also need testing in real learning platforms.

This preview therefore describes a direction, not a promised release. If the package is approved, teachers should receive a coherent, versioned starting point rather than a collection of disconnected files.
